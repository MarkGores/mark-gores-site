How I Work

I've watched people buy at the top, panic at the bottom, and stretch into mortgages that kept them up at night.

Twenty-two years of that changes how you do this job. I'm not trying to close you. I'm trying to make sure you're still happy with this decision in five years.

I live here. My kids go to school here. I'll see you at Lakefront Park or the grocery store. I'd like that to be a good conversation.

I'll respond fast

When people work with me, they feel like they're my only client. I pick up the phone. I answer the text. I don't let things sit.

I won't hound you

No drip campaigns. No "just checking in!" emails. When you're ready, I'm here. When you're not, I'll respect that.

I know the deals

Not the scripts — the deals. Inspections, negotiations, contingencies, title issues. The stuff that matters when money is on the line.

I'll be honest

Even when it costs me a commission. Especially then. You're making the biggest purchase of your life — you deserve someone in your corner, not on a sales floor.
